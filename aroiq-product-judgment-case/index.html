When the numbers reconcile but I still would not ship

A small case I built after studying Arolytics' public Product Manager, AroIQ role. The goal is not to reproduce AroIQ. It is to show how I think about customer-data acceptance, traceability, uncertainty and human judgement.

Candidate: Kirill Derhachenko · Calgary, AB
No Arolytics or customer data was used. All facilities, tags, events, volumes and thresholds are synthetic.

The product question

Data Engineering can make a pipeline run and Data Science can make a model produce an output. Product still has to decide:

Is this customer's data trustworthy enough to become product truth?

Flow of truth

Customer SCADA→Semantic mapping→Model outputs→Event contract→Rollups→Operator view / export

Decision: REVIEW — do not accept yet

The historical reference events and rollups reconcile within the synthetic tolerance. But one SCADA tag mapping — PILOT_A → flare_pilot_status — is still awaiting operator confirmation.

Why stop? A numerically plausible result can still be semantically wrong if the source signal does not mean what we think it means.

What I would resolve before ship

Operator / customer SME
What physical state does the tag represent? Can it be stale, bypassed or inverted? Did instrumentation change during the reference period?
Data Engineering
Is the mapping versioned and reproducible? How are late records and tag renames handled? Can mapping changes restate history?
Data Science / emissions
Does the tag affect detection, causation, quantification or only context? How should unresolved semantics affect uncertainty?
Product / Engineering
Which changes are release blockers? Can users see why a value changed? Are detection, causation and quantification confidence kept separate?

Where AI helps — and where I stop it

Use AI for: profiling exports, candidate mappings, anomaly detection, draft transforms, test generation, reference comparisons and difference summaries.

Keep human-owned: physical tag meaning, material exceptions, unresolved semantic uncertainty and final customer-data acceptance.

Why this case

Arolytics publicly describes this role as owning acceptance criteria, UAT, customer onboarding acceptance, data contracts, reference datasets, regression checks and the final judgement on what ships. I chose to demonstrate that judgement rather than create a UI mock-up or pretend to reproduce their emissions models.
